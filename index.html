ABOUT SMART CITY DELHI AIRPORT

SMART CITY DELHI AIRPORT SMART CITY DELHI AIRPORT is a visionary urban landscape on Dwarka Expressway. It traverses through sector 111, 113 and 114 of Gurugram. It is conceptualised with inspiration from 12 global iconic cities with environment consciousness, cutting edge technology and thought to heightened quality of life at its core. SCDC borders Delhi and is 7 minutes’ drive from the Indira Gandhi International Airport.
The city will have mixed land use to facilitate offices, retail, hospitality and residential all complimenting each other seamlessly to create a self-sustaining ecosystem. The frontage of about 1.5 Km on Dwarka expressway will be used for commercial establishments with an assortment of high street retail, luxury retail and grade A offices. The typical design language will be modern and highly stylised skyscrapers akin to Manhattan style skyline. Residential mix is envisioned to have high rise apartments, villas, penthouses, sky villas, golf theme residences, water theme residences.
Smart City Delhi Airport will infuse elements to reduce carbon footprint and make everyday living sustainable.
